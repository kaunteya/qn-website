# Upgrading to PRO

Upgrading to PRO removes all the limits imposed in the free version.

## Upgrading to PRO gives you
- Unlimited notes
- Unlimited days of use
- iCloud sync
- Folders to organize your notes

## One purchase for all your devices
Quick Note PRO covers your Mac, iPhone and iPad together. Buy it once on any of them, and it unlocks on every device signed in to the same Apple Account. There's no need to buy it again on each device.

## How to Upgrade to Pro
**On Mac**
1. Open app menu
2. Click on 'Upgrade to Pro' to open Purchase window
3. Click on buy. Confirm the price and approve the transaction

**On iPhone and iPad**
1. Go to the sidebar, the list of folders. On iPhone, tap the back button at the top-left of your notes to reach it
2. Tap 'Upgrade to Pro' at the bottom of the sidebar to open the Purchase screen
3. Tap on buy. Confirm the price and approve the transaction

## FAQ
### Is Quick Note Pro a subscription?
NO. Quick Note Pro is a one-time purchase now and forever

### I bought PRO on my Mac. Do I have to buy it again on my iPhone?
No. Open the Purchase screen on your iPhone or iPad and tap `Restore Purchase`. PRO unlocks right away, as long as the device is signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.

## Note:
- You should have appropriate payment enabled in your Apple Account
- For parental permission, you purchase will reflect automatically after approval
