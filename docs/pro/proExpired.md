# Pro version expired

Quick Note Pro is a one-time purchase. Once purchased, you can use it forever, on your Mac, iPhone and iPad.

In a rare case, if Quick Note is not able to load your PRO purchase, then it may suggest you to 'Upgrade to Pro'.
In such scenario please consider the following to restore your Purchase

## Restore Purchase
1. Open the Purchase window from 'Upgrade to Pro'
2. Click on `Restore Purchase`

Make sure the device is signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you bought PRO with.

## Restart the app
**On Mac**
1. Open the main menu
2. Goto `More` section and click on `Quit`
3. Launch Quick Note again.

**On iPhone and iPad**
1. Open the App Switcher by swiping up from the bottom of the screen and pausing
2. Swipe up on Quick Note to close it
3. Open Quick Note again.

After launching the app, the 'Upgrade to Pro' prompt should disappear

## Reinstall the app (Mac only)
If restarting didn't work, then you can consider reinstalling the app on your Mac. Reinstalling your app will keep your notes intact.
To reinstall the app
1. Goto `Finder`
2. Goto `Applications` folder
3. Delete Quick Note app
4. Goto App Store and install Quick Note again

:::warning

Don't delete Quick Note from your iPhone or iPad to fix this. Deleting an app on iPhone and iPad also deletes its data, so any notes that haven't synced to iCloud would be lost.

:::

:::info

  Solutions mentioned above are applicable only when the PRO version has already been purchased.

:::
