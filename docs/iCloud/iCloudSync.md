# iCloud sync

## Backup
iCloud sync will keep all your notes backed up on your iCloud account associated with your Apple Account. Once enabled, all your notes and folders are seamlessly synced on the server.

This feature requires active Internet connection. If your device is not connected to the internet, then backup resumes only after it is connected back to the Internet.

Your backup will be automatically restored when you set up a new Mac, iPhone or iPad with the same Apple Account. This feature is helpful if your device breaks down or gets stolen.

## Sync across devices
With iCloud sync, you can keep your notes synced across your Macs, iPhones and iPads. Write a note on your iPhone and it's waiting on your Mac, and the other way around.
To enable this, install Quick Note on each device and make sure all of them are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on.

If your notes are not showing up on another device, see [Troubleshooting iCloud Sync](./iCloudTroubleShooting.md).
