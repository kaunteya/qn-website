
# Contact Us

For queries, suggestions and issues, drop an email at k.suryawanshi@gmail.com

### Reporting bug/issue
If you are reporting a bug/issue, then please share the device you are using (Mac, iPhone or iPad), its macOS, iOS or iPadOS version, and the app version in the mail.


:::tip
Please ensure you are using the latest version of the app when reporting any problems.

- **On Mac**, you can [click here](macappstore://itunes.apple.com/app/id1472935217?pt=109796802&ct=website-download-button&mt=8) to check if there is an app update available.
- **On iPhone and iPad**, [open Quick Note in the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/quick-note-in-the-menu/id1472935217?mt=12) and tap `Update` if it's shown.
:::
