# Welcome to Quick Note

Quick Note is a clutter-free sticky-note app that lets you write a note with just one click, tap or shortcut. It's available on Mac, iPhone and iPad.

- **On Mac**, it's a fast and lightweight menu bar app, optimized for quick note-taking. Notes float on top of all other apps, so they're always visible.
- **On iPhone and iPad**, it's a fast, focused notepad with rich text, folders and instant search.

With iCloud sync, the same notes are on every device you use.

## FAQ

### How can I download the app on iPhone or iPad?
Quick Note is available on iPhone and iPad from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/quick-note-in-the-menu/id1472935217?mt=12). It requires iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later.

It's the same app as on the Mac, so a PRO purchase made on one device unlocks PRO on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Quick Note is **not** available on Android or Windows.

### Quick Note is not visible in the Menu bar
This applies to Quick Note on Mac. If you have a lot of apps in the Menu bar then macOS will manage them by hiding some menubar apps. To fix this, you can quit some existing menubar apps so that there is space for other apps.

### What do I get in the free version?
Quick Note is free to download from the App Store on Mac, iPhone and iPad.
In the free version, you can create up to 6 notes and use the app for 30 days.

### What do I get after upgrading to the PRO version?
Upgrading to PRO will remove all limits imposed by the free version. It's a one-time purchase that covers your Mac, iPhone and iPad. [Show more details](pro/upgradingToPro)

### Can I access my notes on my other Mac, iPhone or iPad?
Yes. Notes sync over iCloud, so sign in to the same Apple Account on each device and install Quick Note on it. See [iCloud sync](iCloud/iCloudSync) for details.
